## Synthetic images (`generated/`)

Every file prefixed `syn_` is **AI-generated** (OpenAI gpt-image-2, generate or edit) and is not a real photo.
`syn_pair<k>_after.jpg` files are AI edits of the matching `before` image. Prompts live in `tools/eval/dataset/make_synthetic.py`.
